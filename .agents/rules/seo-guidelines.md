# SEO Title & Metadata Guidelines

- **Concise SEO Titles**: NEVER make very long SEO titles. Keep titles punchy, high-impact, and strictly under 55–60 characters to prevent Google SERP truncation.
- **Title Formats**:
  - State Hub: `Solar Site Surveyors in Texas | Statewide Coverage`
  - City Pages: `Solar Site Surveyors in [City], TX` (no redundant `| Solar Site Surveyors` or long suffixes).
  - Service Areas: `Service Areas | Solar Site Surveys`
- **Descriptions**: High-converting, focused descriptions around 150–160 characters.
